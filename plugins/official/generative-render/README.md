# Generative Render

Describe a scene and get a picture of your model in it, made by the AI you choose:

- **OpenRouter** (sign in with your browser): one account, many image models (Google's Gemini image models,
  OpenAI's, FLUX, Seedream and more). You pay OpenRouter for what you use.
- **OpenAI** or **Google Gemini** with your own API key.
- **ComfyUI** on your computer or your network: free, private, offline. Works with FLUX.2 klein or FLUX.1 Kontext
  out of the box, or with your own workflow.
- **Free Chat** (the switch at the top of the sheet): takes your view to ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Grok or Meta AI.

**Keep It Exact** (on by default) puts your model's own pixels back over the picture, so the AI can't change
its shape. There are no limits besides your provider's or your computer's.

Your view and your prompt go only to the service you pick; ShapeCraft never sees them.
